A few quick answers.

How do I add NamjoonBot to my server?

Use Add to Discord, choose your server, and follow Discord’s permission prompts. You’ll need permission to add apps to that server.

Can I play or manage my cards here?

Collecting and playing happen inside Discord. This hub is where you can explore rankings, follow events, and find the community.

Join the support server for help, feedback, and suggestions.
